1. The Mystery: What are V4, V12, V14?
The Short Answer: They are encrypted features.

The Student-Friendly Explanation (The "Smoothie" Analogy): Imagine you have a list of sensitive customer data: Location, Device Type, IP Address, User Age, and Spending History.

Banks cannot give this raw data to researchers because it violates privacy laws (GDPR).

So, they use a math trick called PCA (Principal Component Analysis).

Think of it like a blender: They throw all those real features (Location, Age, Device) into a blender and mix them up.

The Result: You get a "Smoothie." You can't point to the "strawberry" (Location) anymore, but the flavor (mathematical pattern) is still there.

V14 might be a mix of Location + Spending Habits.

V4 might be a mix of Time of Day + Device Type.

Why are V14 and V12 important? Even though we don't know their names, the AI learned that Fraudsters have a specific "flavor."

V14 = -15.0 (Very Low): In this dataset, a very low V14 is the strongest signal of a stolen card. It’s like the AI smelling smoke—it doesn't know what is burning, but it knows something is wrong.

Here is the simple, non-math explanation for why we use -16.0 and 400.

1. Why V14 = -16.0? (The "Temperature" Analogy)
Think of the "V" features like the temperature in a room.

The Average (Normal): For most legitimate transactions, the value of V14 is close to 0. (Imagine 0°C is a comfortable normal day in this math world).

The Range: Most normal people fluctuate between -2 and +2.

The Anomaly: Suddenly, you see a value of -16.0.

That is extremely far from 0. It’s like the temperature suddenly dropping to -50°C.

Because it is so rare and extreme, the AI marks it as "Suspicious."

Scientific Answer for Faculty:

"Sir, the features V1 to V28 have been standardized. This means '0' is the average. A value of '-16.0' is a statistical outlier—it is 16 standard deviations away from the norm. In this dataset, such extreme negative values are almost exclusively found in fraud cases."

2. Why Time = 400? (The "Burst" Factor)
What 'Time' means: It is not 4:00 AM. It is the number of seconds since the dataset recording started.

Why 400?

Time = 400 means this transaction happened very early, just 400 seconds (about 6 minutes) after the first recorded transaction.

The Pattern: Fraudsters often use automated bots. These bots try to run thousands of transactions very quickly (in a short time).

If you enter a random time like 400 or 10, it simulates a transaction happening in a specific "cluster" of activity.

Why isn't it as important as V14?

Time matters less than V14. You could change Time to 5000 and the model might still say Fraud if V14 is -16. But we use 400 to keep the test case consistent.

3. Why V4 = 9.0? (The Opposite Direction)
Remember your Heatmap? The V4 bar went to the Right (Positive).

This means for V4, Higher is Bad.

Normal V4: Around 0.

Fraud V4: High positive numbers (like +9.0).

Why we combine them:

A thief looks like this: (V14 is Extremely Low) + (V4 is Extremely High).

This specific combination is the "fingerprint" of the crime.
